## 2. Top 5 clusters

1. **Планер — по конкретному месяцу/году** (планер июнь/сентябрь 2026, планер на 2027 год). This is the highest-leverage cluster to automate: one HTML→PDF template with month/year as the only variable, recurring monthly demand confirmed in both engines, and it naturally extends into the "календарь 2027" cluster for a year-end content wave. Low production cost per unit, near-zero creative risk.

2. **Трекер привычек**. Dense, consistent suggestions across both engines with clear format sub-intents (а4/а5, черно-белый, для детей, 30 дней, pdf/habit tracker). It is evergreen with a January spike, cheap to produce (grid + icons), and has a distinct less-competitive kids sub-niche. Strong Pinterest fit since habit-tracker aesthetics are a well-established pin category.

3. **Шаблон открытки / шаблон приглашения на день рождения**. Both are dense, holiday- and event-anchored (23 Feb, 8 Mar, 9 May, birthdays by age/gender), which gives a ready-made annual content calendar and strong Pinterest virality (seasonal card/invite pins perform reliably). Free stock photos + Claude-written copy cover the creative need well, and gendered/age-segmented invites let you multiply SKUs cheaply.

4. **Чек-лист сумка в роддом**. Very dense suggestion list, year-tagged (2025/2026) confirming an annual refresh cycle, high emotional urgency (strong lead-magnet conversion), and an underserved "на кесарево" sub-variant. Evergreen, birth-rate-driven demand with no real seasonality risk.

5. **Меню на неделю (+ список покупок)**. The single densest cluster observed, with rich diet-specific long-tail (диабет, похудение, ПП, заболевания печени) that signals both volume and higher purchase intent for niche variants. Pairing with список покупок as a bundle add-on increases average order value without extra creative cost; January spike aligns with New Year health resolutions.

## 3. Notable methodology findings
- **Homonym trap**: "купить планер" surfaces glider-aircraft ("самолет", "с мотором", "бланик") results almost exclusively — this term is not usable for purchase-intent keyword targeting in this niche.
- **Short/weak seeds**: "чек-лист сборы в поездку", "чек-лист ремонта квартиры", "финансовый планер", "планирование свадьбы", and "шаблон бирки для подарка" all returned very short (1-3 item) suggestion lists in both engines — likely lower real search volume, or the true volume sits under adjacent phrasings not tested here (e.g. "список гостей на свадьбу", "смета ремонта квартиры").
- **Recurring year-tags** (2025/2026 in роддом lists, 2026/2027 in планер/календарь lists) confirm these are queries users re-search annually — good candidates for a templated, auto-refreshed content pipeline rather than one-off posts.
- **"Вишлист" vs "wish list"**: transliterated "вишлист" dominates over the English "wish list" in both suggestion sets — use the Cyrillic transliteration as the primary keyword.
