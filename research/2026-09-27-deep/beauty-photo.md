# Beauty / Photo Ideas — RU Query Research (2026-09-27)

Method: Yandex (`suggest.yandex.ru/suggest-ff.cgi`) and Google (`suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search`) autocomplete, ~63 fetches, breadth-first from seeds in маникюр/ногти, причёски, макияж, образы/капсульный гардероб, позы/идеи для фото. Seeds expanded with modifiers (season, occasion, age/figure, "пинтерест", "эстетика", "простой", "для начинающих", "распечатать", "чек-лист").

Scoring:
- **Pinterest-fit / Printable-fit**: 1–5, gut signal from query shape and stock-photo/blog fit.
- **Claude-producible**: 1–5, honest score for an AI with only text + code + free stock photos (Unsplash/Pexels), **no original photography/illustration and no copying other people's photos**. Nail/hair/face close-ups score low almost by definition; text/table/checklist/vector-stencil deliverables score high.

## Top 5 clusters (weighted for Claude-producibility, not just demand)

1. **Капсульный гардероб — чек-листы/планеры/таблицы (printable)** (#16): Demand is real but modest in raw volume; producibility is the whole point — a wardrobe planner, 30-day outfit grid, or packing checklist is pure text/table/PDF layout generated by code, with zero photography risk. This is the single cleanest match between the niche and Claude's actual capabilities.
2. **Трафареты/шаблоны для дизайна ногтей (printable)** (#5): Explicit "распечатать" intent plus appearance in both Yandex and Google autocomplete. Geometric/French-tip/stripe nail stencils are original vector shapes — sellable as a printable PDF pack without any nail photography.
3. **Стиль/эстетика — определение (Pinterest identity content)** (#19): Lower-volume but purely informational ("что такое стиль пинтерест") — a text explainer article needs no photography at all and is cheap evergreen SEO/blog filler.
4. **Образы/look идеи по сезону, в т.ч. с Wildberries** (#18): Strongest organic affiliate signal in the whole dataset — users are already typing "образы ... с вб" into the search box. Outfit-to-WB-SKU text guides (with WB's own product photos via affiliate embed, not our photography) are viable and monetizable.
5. **Что носить / стиль по фигуре и возрасту** (#17): High long-tail volume across age/body-type combinations, and the deliverable is advice text ("что скрыть/подчеркнуть" for a given figure) rather than photos of a specific styled outfit — pairs naturally with generic stock or affiliate product images instead of original photography.
