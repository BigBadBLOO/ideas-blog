## 2. Top 5 clusters (rationale)

1. **Трекер привычек — шаблон/распечатать (#11).** The suggestion list is dense with explicit commercial modifiers (скачать, распечатать, формат а4/а5, excel, word, гугл таблицы), which is a much stronger buying signal than the teen-heavy drawing clusters. The content itself — a grid/table with day numbers and habit rows — is close to 100% producible with text and code, no original art needed, and the audience (adults doing self-improvement/productivity planning) has real disposable income for a paid PDF pack.

2. **Ежедневник — идеи оформления + шаблоны разворотов (#10).** Second-highest printable-fit score and a direct query for "шаблоны разворотов" (spread templates), which is literally the product format we'd sell. Demand is broad (oformlenie, что писать, в клетку, на осень) and stable across both engines, making it a safe pillar with many long-tail sub-pages.

3. **Что нарисовать (общее + когда скучно) (#5).** By raw suggestion count this is the single largest signal in the whole dataset — it dominates nearly every seed variant in both engines. "100 things to draw" prompt lists are exactly the AI-native content format described in the brief, and even though the buying audience is mostly teens with low spend, the traffic/ad and Pinterest-funnel value alone justifies it as a cornerstone pillar page.

4. **ЛД / личный дневник — идеи и оформление (#1).** The abbreviation "лд" plus the full "личный дневник" phrasing together produced the widest breadth of modifiers of any topic (пинтерест эстетика, первая страница, для девочек, легкие, на каждый день), and it's the entry point that also surfaces the blogger-brand cluster (#2), confirming there's an established, hungry audience actively consuming this exact content type, even if via named creators.

5. **Скрапбукинг — идеи (#20).** Less voluminous than the top four but stands out for pairing a strong Pinterest-fit (5) with strong printable-fit (5): digital scrapbook kits (papers, stickers, journaling cards) are a natural paid-PDF product, the audience skews adult women with real spending power, and "фотоальбом"/"блокнот" sub-intents show gift-season crossover potential.
