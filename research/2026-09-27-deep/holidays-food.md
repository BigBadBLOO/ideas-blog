# Праздники и праздничный стол — deep query research (2026-09-27)

Method: Yandex (`suggest.yandex.ru/suggest-ff.cgi`) and Google (`suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox`) autocomplete, breadth-first from seed phrases ("идеи <тема>", "что приготовить на …", "как оформить …", "сервировка …"), expanded with modifiers (год, для кого, недорого, своими руками, распечатать, шаблон). ~65 fetches across both engines. All queries below are verbatim autocomplete suggestions — nothing invented.

Stale-cache note: suggestions containing **2025** are leftover from last season's cache (Yandex/Google haven't fully rotated to 2027 yet); **2026** is the current/upcoming year for most of these holidays (New Year's Eve 2026→2027, Easter 2026 etc.) and is the freshest signal; **2027** appears already for New Year queries — strong forward-looking signal, content should target 2027 for New Year.

## 2. Top 5 clusters

1. **Новогодний стол — идеи/оформление/сервировка** — the single strongest, cleanest demand signal in the whole run (top suggestion across independent seeds in both engines), squarely visual/Pinterest-native, and producible today with stock photography + text guides. The "для двоих" sub-intent is a distinct, underserved angle worth its own pin/board.

2. **Печатные материалы (планер/чек-лист/рассадка/открытки/этикетки/бирки/снежинки/трафареты)** — matches the business model exactly: every item is a text-and-vector artifact Claude can generate without any photography, "распечатать"/"шаблон" surfaced across nearly every seed tried, and it monetizes directly as sellable PDFs.

3. **Конкурсы и игры для праздника** — highest Claude-producible score in the whole set (pure game/text design), strong demand from both "день рождения" and "новый год" contexts independently, and converts naturally into a sellable printable pack (bingo cards, question cards) even though its raw Pinterest-fit is low.

4. **Пасха — стол/меню/яйца/кулич** — dense cross-engine demand across every sub-intent (decor, menu, egg painting, kulich), high visual appeal, and its printable angle (egg-painting templates, coloring pages) is genuinely code/vector-producible without original photography.

5. **Пикник — еда и сервировка** — long, clean suggestion lists from both engines including a query that literally contains "пинтерест," a 3-4 month seasonal window (easier content cadence than one-day holidays), and a checklist-centric format that sidesteps the food-photography weakness of other food clusters.
